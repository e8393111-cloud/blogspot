## ★공통 금지 (프롬프트마다 반복)
```
NO panel, NO glass, NO translucent box, NO rounded card, NO white or ivory block,
NO frosted overlay strip, NO 50/50 frame split, NO icon badges, NO circular sticker
or emoji icons, NO dotted separator lines, NO white or foggy wash over the photo,
NO template-like side panel. ONE continuous full-bleed photograph fills the entire
frame and stays crisp. Place the Korean text DIRECTLY on the naturally bright,
uncluttered area with NO shadow, NO outline and NO glow.
```
